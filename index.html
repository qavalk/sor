Our Story

Forged in Flame,
Rooted in Rome

Roman is the collaboration between Pretty Decent Concepts and James Beard Award-winning chef Scott Conant: a restaurant that celebrates centuries-old techniques and the art of cooking over flame.

From Roman street croquettes and Lingua di Pizza to hand-made pasta and steaks over white oak, every plate passes through the fire.
